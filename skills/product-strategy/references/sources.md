# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes from,
and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Rumelt, *Good Strategy / Bad Strategy*: the kernel (diagnosis, guiding
  policy, coherent action) and the diagnostics for bad strategy in §3.
- Martin and Lafley, *Playing to Win*: the where-to-play and how-to-win
  cascade, and the "what would have to be true" inversion in §7.
- Wodtke, *Radical Focus*: why goals restated as strategy leave teams with
  targets and no approach.
- Doerr, *Measure What Matters*: the goal artifact §2 separates from strategy.
