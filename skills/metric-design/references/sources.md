# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes from,
and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Amplitude, *The North Star Playbook*: one output metric with a small set of
  movable inputs beneath it, the structure of §3 and §4.
- Wodtke, *Radical Focus*: key results as results rather than deliverables, and
  the so-what discipline in §7.
- Rodden, Hutchinson and Fu, *Measuring the User Experience on a Large Scale*
  (the HEART framework and its goals-signals-metrics step): the
  experience-quality escape hatch in §3.
- Goodhart, *Problems of Monetary Management*: the law behind §5.
- Kohavi, Tang and Xu, *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*: power,
  interference, and the cases against testing in §8.
