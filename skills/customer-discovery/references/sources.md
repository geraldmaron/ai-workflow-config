# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes from,
and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits*: the weekly touchpoint, the product
  trio, and the opportunity solution tree behind §2 and §6.
- Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*: the question discipline in §4: past behavior
  over future intent, compliments as noise, commitment as the real signal.
- Portigal, *Interviewing Users*: moderation conduct, silence, and rapport.
- Glaser and Strauss, *The Discovery of Grounded Theory*: theoretical
  saturation, the origin of the per-segment stopping rule in §5.
