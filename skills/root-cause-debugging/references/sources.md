# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. Reading these is not required to follow
the skill:

- Popper, *The Logic of Scientific Discovery*: falsification over
  confirmation, which is the whole of §5.
- Agans, *Debugging: The 9 Indispensable Rules for Finding Even the Most
  Elusive Software and Hardware Problems*: quit thinking and look; change one
  thing at a time.
- Zeller, *Why Programs Fail: A Guide to Systematic Debugging*: delta
  debugging and the discipline of narrowing a reproduction.
- Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, CIA Center for the Study of
  Intelligence, 1999: competing hypotheses weighed by disconfirmation.
- Dekker, *The Field Guide to Understanding Human Error*: why the first
  plausible cause is usually where the search stopped, not where it should
  have.
