# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes
from, and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Watkins, *The First 90 Days*: the STARS classification, the transition
  phases, and early wins as credibility rather than value.
- Klein, *Performing a Project Premortem*, Harvard Business Review, 2007:
  the prospective-hindsight technique behind gate 9.
- Kotter, *Power and Influence*: decision rights and informal networks as
  distinct from the reporting chart.
- Cross & Parker, *The Hidden Power of Social Networks*: the method behind
  finding trusted nodes by asking who people check with.
