# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes
from, and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Klein, *Performing a Project Premortem*, Harvard Business Review, 2007:
  the prospective-hindsight technique behind gate 10.
- Reason, *Managing the Risks of Organizational Accidents*: layered defenses
  and why a skipped control is only visible after it was needed.
- Forsgren, Humble & Kim, *Accelerate*: progressive delivery, change failure
  rate, and time to restore as the properties a rollout mechanic is chosen for.
- Beyer et al., *Site Reliability Engineering*, chapters on release engineering
  and postmortem culture: error budgets as go/no-go criteria, and reviews that
  end in a decision.
