# Sources

Method identified, not incorporated. These name where the discipline comes
from, and reading them is not required to follow it:

- Klein, *Sources of Power*, and *Performing a Project Premortem*, Harvard
  Business Review, 2007: reasoning about what did not happen, and prospective
  hindsight, behind sections 7 and 9.
- Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, CIA Center for the Study of
  Intelligence, 1999: disconfirmation over confirmation, behind the overclaim
  check.
- Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow*: hindsight bias and narrative coherence,
  the two forces that make a reconstructed period read cleaner than it was.
- Rubin, *Estimating Causal Effects of Treatments*, Journal of Educational
  Psychology, 1974: the counterfactual framing section 6 borrows in plain
  language, without claiming its rigor.
